01 — About

Engineer across data, .NET, DevOps & AI

Engineer with more than four years in financial services at ODDO BHF, working across data, .NET, DevOps, AI and IT service management. On the OBAM team I led the migration of 2000+ SSIS packages from on-premises to a modern stack, built ELIOT reporting and FxTrades integrations, and shipped the ESG platform that surfaces key indicators to the business.

Today I split my time between the TopEase CMDB project and an ISM security program: monitoring production environments, firewalls, DNS, SSL/TLS configuration, and delivering Control-M-driven reporting to the business. I also built an LLM assistant on Ivanti HEAT, and I've mentored two alternance students into the team, teaching ETL and ISM hands-on. 🤖
